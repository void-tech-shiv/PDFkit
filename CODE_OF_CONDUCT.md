# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

PDFkit aims to be a respectful, welcoming place for people to collaborate, learn, and improve document tools. We want participation to be free from harassment and personal attacks, regardless of experience level, background, identity, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that helps create a positive environment:

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Give and receive feedback professionally.
- Assume good intent while addressing the impact of words or actions.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, and behavior—not on people.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's personal information, private documents, credentials, or private communications.
- Help keep discussions relevant and accessible to people with different experience levels.

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples include harassment, intimidation, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, personal attacks, sexualized attention without consent, doxxing, and publishing private or confidential information.

## Maintainer response

The project maintainer is responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code of Conduct in project spaces. The maintainer may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation for behavior that violates these standards.

If the concern involves the maintainer, or you do not feel comfortable raising it in a public issue, contact the maintainer through the [GitHub profile](https://github.com/void-tech-shiv) and ask to discuss it privately. Please do not include sensitive personal information in a public report.

The maintainer will make a reasonable effort to review reports and respond appropriately, but this project does not currently promise a response time or provide a separate moderation team.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces where someone is representing PDFkit or its community.

## Attribution

This is a project-specific code of conduct written for PDFkit. It is informed by common open-source community practices.
